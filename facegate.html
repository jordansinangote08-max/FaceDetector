    How it stays accurate over time. Each capture becomes a 128-number descriptor — a mathematical fingerprint, not a photo. When someone is recognised with high confidence, Facegate quietly saves that fresh descriptor too, so the profile keeps up as hair, weight, glasses and age change.

    What honest accuracy looks like. No face recognition system is 100% correct — not this one, not the ones on phones. Expect roughly 95–99% on a small roster in decent light. It gets worse with harsh backlight, heavy motion blur, masks, or identical twins. Treat a match as strong evidence, never as proof.

    Where your data lives. Descriptors are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key stretched from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 250,000 rounds) and stored only in this browser. There is no account, no server, no upload. Someone who steals the device still faces the encryption — but keep the passphrase strong, because that is the whole lock.
